# Demo room images

These sample interiors are for demonstration and do not depict the seeded rooms. Originals from Unsplash:

- `interior.jpg`: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1615749250437-8f3fd29f9118

Run `flask --app app seed-room-photos` to fill empty slots only. Each room gets its own copies so replacing/deleting one room's photos does not affect another room. Files with `demo-` names are identified as sample photos in the room view.
